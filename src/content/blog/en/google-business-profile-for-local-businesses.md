When someone searches for "graphic design in Santa Cruz" or "garage near me", the first thing they usually see isn't a website but a map with several businesses and their listings. That listing is your Google Business Profile, and for many local businesses it's the main way new customers find them. It's free, but the more complete and up to date it is, the better it works.

## The essentials

- **Your business's real name**, exactly as it appears on your sign. Adding keywords to the name breaks Google's rules and can get the listing suspended.
- **An accurate primary category**: it's one of the factors with the most influence on the searches you appear in. Only add secondary categories if they apply.
- **Address, phone number and opening hours** identical to those on your website and social media.
- **Special opening hours** for bank holidays and holiday periods.
- **A link to your website** or to the page for your most relevant service.

## What makes the difference

1. **Your own recent photos**: the shopfront, the interior, the team and your work. They help people recognise you when they arrive.
2. **Services or products** with a short description and, if you can, an indicative price.
3. **Regular posts**: news, offers or events keep the listing active.
4. **Questions and answers**: answer the most common questions yourself before others do it for you.
5. **Reviews**: ask satisfied customers for their opinion and reply to all of them, including the negative ones, politely and with a proposed solution.

## Consistency everywhere

Google and AI assistants check your details against other sources: directories, social networks and third-party websites. If your address or phone number appears in different forms, trust drops. Checking that consistency is part of local [[SEO|seo]] work and of [[LLMO|llmo]] too, because [[language models|llm]] rely on that information when they talk about your business.

## Common mistakes

- Opening several listings for the same business.
- Not updating the opening hours after a change.
- Leaving reviews unanswered for months.
- Using stock photos instead of real images.

## How to tell whether it's working

The profile itself shows how many people have viewed it, how many have called, asked for directions or visited your website. Check it every month and compare it with previous months to see the trend.

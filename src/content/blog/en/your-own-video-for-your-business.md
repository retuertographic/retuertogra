A photo shows what your business looks like; a video shows how it works. You see the people, hear how they speak, understand the process. That's why your own video is one of the most direct ways to show what makes you different, something a stock image library can never tell for you.

## What you can film

Most useful business videos fall into four types:

- **Interviews.** The founder, a team member or a customer describing their experience. They give the brand a face and a voice.
- **Products.** What it looks like, how it's used, what details it has. Very useful for shops, manufacturers and restaurants.
- **Spaces.** The premises, the terrace, the workshop, the rooms or the views. Ideal for hospitality, accommodation and shops with a place worth seeing.
- **Processes.** How an order is prepared, how a product is made or how work is carried out on site. It explains without long texts and conveys craftsmanship.

## What each video is for

| Use | Example |
| --- | --- |
| Website | A short introduction video on the home page or a service page |
| Social media | Short vertical or square pieces, each with one message |
| Campaigns | Ads with one clear idea and an ending that invites action |
| Sales | A video explaining your service before a meeting |

A video on a [[landing page|landing-page]] helps visitors quickly understand what you offer, and good footage strengthens your [[branding|branding]] across every channel.

## When a professional shoot makes sense

A phone is fine for everyday content: stories, quick announcements, spontaneous moments. A professional shoot makes sense when the video will represent your business for some time:

1. **You're redoing your website** or launching a service and want to present it well.
2. **You're about to invest in advertising** and need pieces that live up to the budget.
3. **Your value lies in what a photo can't show**: the service, the process, the place.
4. **You want material for months**: a single shoot can produce many different pieces.

## What to be clear about before you call

- **The goal**: raising awareness, explaining, selling or attracting talent.
- **Where it will be seen**: website, social media, campaigns or all of them.
- **Who appears**: team members who feel comfortable in front of the camera.
- **Your look**: colours, logo and style, so the video is consistent with your [[visual identity|identidad-visual]].

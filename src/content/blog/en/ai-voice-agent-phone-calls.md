An unanswered call is usually a customer dialling the next number on the list. And answering the phone all day constantly interrupts the work of garages, clinics, restaurants or professional firms. An [[AI voice agent|agente-voz]] makes sure every call gets an answer without anyone having to drop what they are doing.

## What exactly it is

It's a phone assistant built on a [[large language model|llm]] that understands what the caller says and replies in a natural voice. Unlike "press 1, press 2" switchboards, it holds a conversation: it understands questions phrased in many ways, asks for missing details and confirms what it has understood.

## Which tasks it can take on

- **Basic information**: opening hours, address, services, indicative prices or documents required.
- **Bookings and appointments**: it checks availability, suggests slots and records the appointment.
- **Taking details**: who is calling, what they need and how to reach them.
- **Transfers**: it passes the call to the right person when the case requires it or the customer asks.
- **Logging**: each call is summarised in your [[CRM|crm]] or sent to you by email, so nobody has to take notes.

## Which businesses benefit most

It works best where many calls are similar: clinics and beauty centres managing appointments, garages updating customers on a vehicle, restaurants taking bookings, professional firms or technical services receiving call-outs. If almost all your calls are different and complex, the agent will mainly filter and take details, and it's worth planning it that way from the start.

## What to prepare before switching it on

1. **A list of the usual questions** with correct, up-to-date answers.
2. **Transfer rules**: which cases always go to a person and at what times.
3. **Access to your calendar** or booking system through an [[integration|integracion]], if it will manage appointments.
4. **The tone**: how it greets, introduces itself and says goodbye. Telling callers they're speaking to an automated assistant builds more trust than hiding it.

## Privacy and transparency

Calls contain personal data, so the system must comply with the [[GDPR|rgpd]]: inform callers at the start, keep only what is needed and only for as long as necessary. It's part of the design, not an afterthought.

## How to measure the result

Compare before and after: missed calls, appointments booked and time your team spends on the phone. Within a few weeks you'll have enough data to fine-tune answers and hours.

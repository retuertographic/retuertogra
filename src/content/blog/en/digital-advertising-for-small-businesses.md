Digital advertising lets your business appear in front of people who don't know you yet, at the time and place you choose. But it's also a very quick way to spend money if it's launched without strategy or measurement. Before switching on your first campaign, it's worth answering three questions: what for, for whom, and how you'll know whether it works.

## When advertising makes sense

- **You have a clear product or service** and know who it's for.
- **You want short-term results**: filling a season, launching something new or winning enquiries while your [[SEO|seo]] matures.
- **You can handle what comes in.** Attracting enquiries is pointless if nobody replies in time.
- **You can measure.** If you don't know how many enquiries or sales each campaign generates, you can't improve it.

If any of these points is missing, sort it out before investing.

## What to have ready first

1. **A prepared destination.** A clear website or a campaign-specific [[landing page|landing-page]] that loads quickly and explains the offer.
2. **An easy way to get in touch**: a form, a WhatsApp button, a phone number or online booking.
3. **Measurement set up**, to record which visits turn into enquiries or sales.
4. **A specific goal**: more calls, more bookings, more online shop sales or more quote requests.
5. **A test budget** you can afford without relying on it working from day one.

## Which platform suits your business

| Platform | When it usually fits |
| --- | --- |
| Google Ads | When people are already searching for what you offer: "plumber in La Laguna", "car hire in south Tenerife" |
| Meta Ads (Facebook and Instagram) | When you want to reach an audience by area, age or interests |
| TikTok Ads | When your product comes across well in vertical video and your audience is there |
| LinkedIn Ads | When you sell to other businesses or to specific professional profiles |

On Google the key is choosing each [[keyword|palabra-clave]] well; on social networks, targeting and the ad creative. You don't need to be everywhere: start with the best fit and expand once you have data.

## Think about the whole journey

Not everyone buys on seeing the first ad. Some people are discovering your brand, some are comparing and some are ready to decide. Thinking of advertising as a [[funnel|embudo]] helps you give each person the right message at the right moment, instead of asking for the sale from someone who has only just met you.

## Start small and measure

Launch a focused campaign with a clear goal and message, and review the results calmly before raising the budget. Choose a few [[KPIs|kpi]], such as cost per enquiry or per sale, and compare them with what each customer brings you.

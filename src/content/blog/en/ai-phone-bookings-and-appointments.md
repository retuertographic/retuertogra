Managing appointments and bookings is one of the tasks where an [[AI voice agent|agente-voz]] adds the most value, because the calls follow a pattern: someone wants a slot, they're offered options and the details are confirmed. But every business has its own quirks, and a good result depends on preparing for them before switching the system on.

## What it needs in every case

- **Access to the real diary** through an [[integration|integracion]] with your appointment or booking system, so it only offers slots that are actually free.
- **Clear rules**: how long each service takes, gaps between appointments, blocked days and minimum notice.
- **Which details to ask for**: name, phone number and just enough to prepare the appointment. The fewer the questions, the fewer the mistakes.
- **Confirmation**: repeating the day, time and service out loud, and sending a confirmation message or email.
- **When to hand over to a person**: emergencies, complicated changes or customers who ask for it.

## Clinics and beauty salons

Here the important thing is to assign the right type of appointment and the right professional. The agent should ask about the reason in general terms and leave the clinical details for the appointment itself. It also needs to handle cancellations and changes, which are frequent in this kind of business. Because sensitive data is involved, [[GDPR|rgpd]] compliance must be built in from the design stage: inform callers at the start, ask only for what's necessary and keep no more than is strictly needed.

## Garages

At a garage, many calls are to book a service or to ask how a repair is going. The agent can take the registration number, the model and the type of work, and offer a slot based on how busy the workshop is. For status enquiries, it needs access to the management system or, at the very least, the ability to take a note and let the mechanic in charge know.

## Restaurants

Table bookings have their own rules: number of diners, sittings, terrace or indoors, special requests and large groups. The agent must know the real capacity and recognise when a group needs the manager's confirmation. Out of hours, being able to book by phone at any time is a clear advantage over an answering machine.

## Test before opening it to customers

Make test calls with your team, imitating real customers: those who talk fast, those who change their minds, those who don't know which service they need. Adjust the replies and the rules until the conversation flows. Then, for the first few weeks, review every call logged in your [[CRM|crm]].

Sending an email campaign is easy; getting it opened, read and acted on is another matter. Most problems crop up again and again from one business to the next, and they can be fixed. Here are the most common mistakes and how to measure results so you improve with every send.

## Common mistakes

1. **Emailing people who didn't ask.** Using bought lists or addresses collected without consent conflicts with the [[GDPR|rgpd]] and damages your reputation as a sender.
2. **Sending the same thing to everyone.** Without segmentation, people receive messages that don't interest them and end up unsubscribing.
3. **Vague subject lines.** The subject line decides whether the email is opened. Specific and honest beats eye-catching and misleading.
4. **Too many messages in one email.** With five offers and six links, nobody knows what to do. One main idea and one clear [[call to action|cta]] button.
5. **Designs that don't work on mobile.** Many emails are read on a phone: the design must be [[responsive|responsive]], with legible text and easy-to-tap buttons.
6. **Everything inside one image.** Some email programs don't show images by default, and the message is lost. Combine text and images.
7. **Irregular frequency.** Going months without writing and then sending three emails in a week is confusing. A steady, reasonable rhythm is better.
8. **Not checking before sending.** Broken links, misspelt names or wrong prices. Always send a test.
9. **Neglecting the technical set-up.** The major mailbox providers are increasingly strict about authenticating the sending [[domain|dominio]] (records such as SPF, DKIM and DMARC). Without it, your campaigns are more likely to end up in the spam folder.

## What to measure after each send

| Indicator | What it shows |
| --- | --- |
| Delivery rate | Whether emails reach the inbox |
| Open rate | How much interest the subject line sparks (indicative) |
| Click rate | Whether the content prompts action |
| Unsubscribes | Whether the frequency or content is tiring people |
| Bounces | The health of your list |
| Conversions | Bookings, purchases or enquiries generated |

Open rates are becoming less accurate, because some email programs download messages automatically to protect their users' privacy. That's why it's better to focus on clicks and, above all, conversions.

## How to improve using the data

- **Choose a few [[KPIs|kpi]]** linked to your goal: if you want bookings, measure bookings, not just opens.
- **Compare similar campaigns** to spot which subject line, day or content works best.
- **Test one thing at a time**, for example two different subject lines with part of the list.
- **Look at the trend**, not a single send: one poor result may be down to the date or a bank holiday.

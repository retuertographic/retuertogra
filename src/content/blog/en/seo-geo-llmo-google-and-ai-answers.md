For years, being visible online meant appearing on the first page of Google. Today many people also ask an artificial intelligence assistant directly — "which design agency is there in Santa Cruz de Tenerife?" — and get a written answer, sometimes with sources cited. To make sure your business appears in both places, it helps to understand three complementary concepts.

## SEO: the foundation hasn't changed

[[SEO|seo]] is the technical and content work needed to appear in search engines' organic results. It includes a fast website that passes the [[Core Web Vitals|core-web-vitals]], looks good on mobile, has a page answering each specific search and earns quality [[backlinks|backlink]] from trustworthy sites. Without this foundation, nothing else holds up.

## GEO: content AI can understand and cite

[[GEO|geo]] aims to get generative engines to use your content when they build their answers. What works best is text that answers directly at the start, is well structured with clear headings, gives concrete facts and makes clear who wrote it and why they know the subject. Well-written FAQs and service pages with prices and conditions help a lot.

## LLMO: how AI talks about your brand

[[LLMO|llmo]] focuses on making sure [[language models|llm]] have correct, consistent information about your business: what you do, where you are, who you work for. That depends on your website, but also on your directory listings, your Google business profile, reviews and mentions in other media. If each source says something different, the AI will hesitate or get it wrong.

## What you can do this month

1. **Check your basic details** (name, address, phone, opening hours) on your website, on Google and in directories. Make sure they match everywhere.
2. **Choose the [[keywords|palabra-clave]] for your main services** and check that each has a page answering it well.
3. **Answer your customers' real questions** on your website, in clear, direct sentences.
4. **Ask happy customers for reviews** and reply to them.
5. **Measure**: enquiries coming from search engines and mentions of your brand. Without measurement you won't know what works.

## A medium-term effort

Ranking isn't a switch: results build up over months and need consistency. The good news is that the three disciplines reinforce each other: useful, well-structured content works for Google and for AI assistants alike.

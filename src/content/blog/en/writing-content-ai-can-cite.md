AI assistants build their answers from content they find and consider reliable. There's no secret formula for appearing in them, but there is a set of good practices that make your content easier to understand, summarise and cite. This is what's known as [[GEO|geo]].

## Answer first, explain afterwards

Assistants look for clear answers. If your page takes four paragraphs to say what you do or how much it costs, it's less likely to be used. Open every page and every section with a sentence that answers the main question directly, then go into the details.

## A structure that speaks for itself

- **One topic per page**: a page for each service, instead of one general page with everything mixed together.
- **Descriptive headings**: each heading should say what the section is about, as if it were a question someone might ask.
- **Lists and tables** for steps, features or comparisons.
- **Short paragraphs**, one idea each.

## Specific, verifiable facts

Generic claims ("we're the best", "quality guaranteed") don't give any information. Specific facts do: what a service includes, usual lead times, starting price, the area you cover and what kind of customer it's for. The more specific you are, the more useful your content will be to the person asking and to the system answering.

## Make it clear who you are

AI systems value knowing who is behind a piece of content. Include who wrote it, the team's experience, where you're based and how to get in touch. Keep those details the same across your whole website and your external profiles.

## Genuine frequently asked questions

A good FAQ section, written from what your customers actually ask, is one of the most useful formats. Each question with a short, direct answer. Avoid filler questions that nobody ever asks.

## The technical basics still count

None of this works if the page is slow to load, doesn't display well on a mobile or can't be crawled. Technical [[SEO|seo]], [[Core Web Vitals|core-web-vitals]] and [[responsive design|responsive]] are still the foundation everything else rests on.

## Measure patiently

Results in AI assistants are harder to measure than on Google. You can regularly ask several assistants questions about your sector and your area, note whether you appear and how you're described, and watch for more enquiries from people who say they found you that way.

A good video starts long before the camera is switched on. A shoot day goes by quickly, and every minute lost looking for a socket, waiting for someone or moving boxes is a shot that doesn't get filmed. With some simple preparation, the same time yields far more material and a better result.

## Before the shoot: decide what you want to say

1. **Set the goal.** Introducing the company, explaining a service, showing a product or preparing ads? A video that tries to say everything says nothing.
2. **Think about where it will be seen.** A website video isn't the same as vertical pieces for social media. If you know the destinations, you can film with all of them in mind.
3. **Write a shot list.** Which spaces, products, processes and people need to appear. It's the basic script for the day.
4. **Prepare the interviews.** Four or five open questions are enough. Share them beforehand so nobody arrives unprepared, but don't ask people to memorise answers: it sounds more natural.
5. **Gather your brand material.** Logo, colours and typefaces, or your [[brand guidelines|manual-marca]] if you have them, so captions and end cards are consistent with your [[visual identity|identidad-visual]].

## The space

- **Tidy and clean** the areas that will appear. The camera sees what you no longer notice: cables, paperwork, old posters.
- **Think about the light.** Tell the crew when your premises get the best natural light. In Tenerife, a terrace at midday and at sunset look completely different.
- **Plan for noise.** Machinery, background music, nearby building work or air conditioning can ruin an interview. If they can be switched off for a while, all the better.
- **Choose the moment.** If you want a lively atmosphere, film during busy hours; if you want clean shots, before opening.

## The people

- **Decide who appears** and confirm they're available that day.
- **Simple clothing**, without fine stripes or other brands' logos. If there's a uniform, make sure it's clean.
- **Ask for permission** from everyone who appears, whether staff or customers. People's data and images must be handled carefully and in line with the [[GDPR|rgpd]].

## On the day

- Someone from your team who knows the business and can answer questions.
- Access to sockets and to the planned spaces.
- Products ready and in good condition.
- Time set aside for each interview, without rushing.
- A final check of the shot list before wrapping up.

## After the shoot

Editing is where the story is built. Review the first cut with one question in mind: is what we wanted to say clear? And check that each piece ends with a clear [[call to action|cta]].

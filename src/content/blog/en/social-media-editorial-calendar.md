Most business accounts aren't abandoned for lack of enthusiasm, but for lack of a plan. There's a flurry of posts one week, nothing the next, and every Monday starts with the same question: "What shall we post today?". An editorial calendar solves that: it decides in advance what gets published, where and when, so your social media no longer depends on spare moments.

## What an editorial calendar is

It's a table, a spreadsheet or a board showing the posts planned for the coming weeks. For each one you note the date, the network, the topic, the format (photo, carousel, video, story) and who is preparing it. You don't need a complicated tool: what matters is that it exists, the whole team can see it and it's actually followed.

## Planning a month step by step

1. **Set two or three goals.** For example, promoting a new service, filling tables on weekdays or getting more enquiries by message. Every post should serve one of them.
2. **Choose your recurring topics.** Four or five categories that repeat: your product or service, the team, useful tips, customer reviews and business news.
3. **Mark the dates that matter.** Seasons, local holidays, events in your area or your own promotions. In the Canary Islands, for instance, Carnival or the change of tourist season can set the tone for a whole month.
4. **Pick a frequency you can keep up.** Posting regularly at a pace you can sustain beats starting strong and then disappearing.
5. **Spread the topics across the weeks** and give each slot a format and a network.
6. **Prepare material in advance.** Photos, copy and designs ready a few days ahead, reviewed and consistent with your [[visual identity|identidad-visual]].

## A minimal template

| Date | Network | Topic | Format | Call to action |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Monday | Instagram | Product of the week | Carousel | Ask us by message |
| Wednesday | Google Business Profile | News or offer | Photo and text | Get directions |
| Friday | Facebook / Instagram | Behind the scenes | Short video | Book or visit |

Note the last column: every post should end with a clear [[call to action|cta]], even a simple one.

## Tips to make the calendar work

- **Leave some slots free** for whatever comes up: a piece of news, a spontaneous photo or a reaction to something happening on your street.
- **Adapt, don't copy.** The same topic can work on several networks, but the copy and format change with each platform.
- **Reuse what you already have.** A question customers often ask can become a useful post.
- **Review at the end of each month** which posts generated the most engagement or enquiries, and adjust the next calendar using those [[KPIs|kpi]].

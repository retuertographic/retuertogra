A new website hardly ever fails because of the design. It fails because of what wasn't decided in time: copy that never arrives, missing photos, vague goals or old pages that vanish without warning. Preparing the project properly before you start saves weeks and makes the result much closer to what you had in mind.

## 1. Define what the website is for

Write down in one sentence what it must achieve: receive quote requests, sell online, take bookings or promote your services. That goal decides which pages matter, where each [[call to action|cta]] goes and what will be measured later.

## 2. Think about who visits it

Are they local customers, tourists, other businesses? Do they arrive on mobile or on a computer? Do you need the site in several languages? In the Canary Islands it's common for part of the audience to arrive in English or German, so it's worth planning for that from the start rather than bolting it on later.

## 3. Take stock of what you already have

If you already have a website, note down:

- Which pages get the most visits and which hardly anyone uses.
- Which forms, downloads or features must be kept.
- Who manages the [[domain|dominio]], the [[hosting|hosting]] and email, and with which logins.

## 4. Gather the content

Content is usually what delays a project most. Prepare in advance:

- Copy for each service, even if it's only a draft.
- Good-quality photos of your own: the team, the premises, the products or your work.
- Logo and brand colours in high resolution.
- Your customers' frequently asked questions, which often become the best content.

## 5. Decide which features you need

Make two lists: what's essential for launch and what can wait. Private customer areas, an online shop, an admin dashboard or connections with other tools should be raised from the start, because they affect the structure.

## 6. Don't lose what already works

If you're replacing a website, the old addresses Google knows about must be redirected to the new ones. It's a key part of technical [[SEO|seo]] and avoids losing traffic that took so much effort to earn.

## Checklist before you start

| Item | Done |
| --- | --- |
| Main goal of the website | ☐ |
| Audience, languages and devices | ☐ |
| Inventory of current pages and features | ☐ |
| Logins for domain, hosting and email | ☐ |
| Copy, photos and logo | ☐ |
| Essential and optional features | ☐ |
| Person responsible for reviewing and approving | ☐ |

With this checklist complete, any quote will be more accurate and development will run more smoothly.

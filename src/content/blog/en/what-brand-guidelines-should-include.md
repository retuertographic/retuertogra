A well-designed logo can lose its strength within months if everyone uses it their own way: stretched on a poster, in another colour on social media or on a background where it can't be read. [[Brand guidelines|manual-marca]] are the document that prevents this wear and tear. They explain how to use the [[visual identity|identidad-visual]] so your business is recognised the same way on a business card, a van or an Instagram post.

## What they are for

- **Consistency**: your brand looks the same whoever produces it, whether your team, a printer or an agency.
- **Time saved**: nobody has to decide each time which colour or typeface to use.
- **Professionalism**: a consistent image conveys order and builds trust.
- **A basis for growth**: new products, premises or campaigns start from clear rules.

## The essential parts

1. **The logo and its versions**: primary, horizontal, vertical, symbol only, and black-and-white or reversed versions.
2. **Clear space and minimum size**: the empty space around the logo and the size below which it stops being legible.
3. **Brand colours**, with their codes for screen (RGB and hex) and print (CMYK and, where relevant, Pantone).
4. **Typefaces**: which ones are used for headings and body text, and fallbacks when they aren't available.
5. **Incorrect uses**: examples of what not to do, such as distorting it, changing colours or adding effects.
6. **Applications**: stationery, email signature, social media, signage, uniforms or vehicles.

## What makes them genuinely useful

Brand guidelines don't need to be a hundred-page book. For a small business, a clear document with visual examples is usually enough, together with the logo files in the right formats (vector for print, PNG or SVG for the web). What matters is that they're at hand for whoever needs them and are updated as the brand evolves.

## More than a logo

[[Branding|branding]] goes beyond graphics: it includes the tone you use with customers and the experience you offer. Some guidelines add writing tips and sample posts, which is especially useful if several people manage your social media.

## When to create them

Now is the time if you're starting your business, refreshing your image or noticing that every graphic comes out different. Also before a major investment in signage, a [[responsive website|responsive]] or campaigns, so everything starts from the same base.

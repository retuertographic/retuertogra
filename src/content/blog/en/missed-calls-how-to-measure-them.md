Almost every business believes it answers most of its calls. Few have measured it. A missed call leaves no trace in the till or the inbox: the customer simply rings somewhere else. That's why it's worth spending a few weeks measuring before deciding whether you need to strengthen your phone service or bring in an [[AI voice agent|agente-voz]].

## Step 1: get the call log

Most phone systems and operators provide a log of incoming calls, with the time and whether they were answered. Ask your provider for it or look for it in your line's online dashboard. If you use mobiles, check each one's call history. You need at least two or three weeks of data for it to be representative.

## Step 2: sort by time of day

Group the unanswered calls into time slots: first thing in the morning, midday, end of the day and out of hours. There are usually clear patterns, such as peaks when the whole team is busy with customers in person, or calls that come in just after closing.

## Step 3: estimate how many were opportunities

Not every call is a sale. Listen to, or ask about, a sample of the calls that were answered and work out what share were new customers, appointments or orders. Apply that proportion to the missed calls: you'll get an estimate of how many opportunities slip away each month.

## Step 4: put a value on it

Multiply those opportunities by the percentage that usually turns into a sale and by the average value of each sale. The result isn't exact, but it gives you an order of magnitude to compare with the cost of the solutions. It's the basis for calculating the [[ROI|roi]] of any change.

## What to do with the results

- **If the losses are concentrated in a few hours**, reorganising shifts may be enough.
- **If they are spread throughout the day or arrive out of hours**, a voice agent can answer, take details and manage appointments without depending on who happens to be free.
- **If many calls are repeated questions** (opening hours, address, prices), automating those answers frees up the team's time even if you don't miss that many calls.

## Measure again

After any change, repeat the measurement over the same period. Comparing before and after is the only way to know whether the investment has paid off. If the voice agent logs every call in your [[CRM|crm]], measuring becomes automatic.
